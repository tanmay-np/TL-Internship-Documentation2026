📘 Day 3: Autodesk Fusion – Sign Up, Install & Basic Modelling

📌 Introduction

Autodesk Fusion is a powerful 3D CAD, CAM, and CAE tool used for product design, mechanical engineering, simulation, and manufacturing. It combines organic shapes modelling, parametric design, and direct editing in one cloud‑based platform – ideal for students, hobbyists, and professionals.

🔄 Step‑by‑Step: Sign Up & Installation

Step 1 – Search Autodesk Fusion

Open Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge and search for “Autodesk Fusion”.

Step 2 – Open Official Website

Click the official Autodesk link (www.autodesk.com).

Step 3 – Click “Get Fusion” or “Free Trial”

Look for the Free Trial, Get Fusion, or Personal Use button.

Step 4 – Choose Your Version

Select Personal Use (Free) or Education Version (for students).

Step 5 – Create Autodesk Account

Fill in first name, last name, email, password, country and click Create Account.

Step 6 – Verify Your Email

Check your inbox for the verification email and click the link.

Step 7 – Sign In to Autodesk

Return to the Fusion download page and sign in with your email and password.

Step 8 – Start Download

Click Download Now – the installer file will be saved.

Step 9 – Open Installer

Go to Downloads folder and double‑click the Fusion installer file.

Step 10 – Install Autodesk Fusion

Follow the installer: accept terms, click Install, wait a few minutes, allow internet access if asked.

Step 11 – Launch Autodesk Fusion

After installation, open Fusion and sign in again. The software is now ready.

⚙️ Basics of Autodesk Fusion – First 3D Model

Step 1 – Open Fusion

Step 2 – Create a new file

Click Create New and choose Part Design.

Step 3 – Create Sketch

Click Create Sketch and select a plane (XY, XZ, or YZ).

Step 4 – Select a plane

Step 5 – Use lines and shapes to make a basic structure

Draw a 2D profile (e.g., rectangle or circle).

Step 6 – Check if shape is closed

Fusion will highlight closed profiles. A closed shape is needed for extrude/revolve.

Step 7 – Revolve (or Extrude)

Go to Create → Revolve or Extrude. Select the profile and axis, adjust angle (360° for revolve).

Step 8 – Hollow the model (Shell)

Use Modify → Shell to remove material from inside and set wall thickness.

Step 9 – Save the model

Click File → Save and give a meaningful name.

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • Autodesk Fusion is free for students and personal use – powerful CAD/CAM/CAE in one tool.
  • The sign‑up process requires an Autodesk account and email verification.
  • Basic modelling workflow: create sketch → select plane → draw closed profile → extrude/revolve → shell (if needed) → save.
  • Fusion supports parametric design, direct editing, and cloud collaboration.
  • It is widely used for 3D printing, CNC machining, product design, and mechanical engineering projects.

📌 Conclusion

Autodesk Fusion is a versatile 3D design software ideal for students, engineers, and designers.
